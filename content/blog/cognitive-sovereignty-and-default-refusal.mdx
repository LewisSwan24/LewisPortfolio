Many AI interfaces optimize for one goal: making every action look effortless. Prompts auto-complete, destructive confirmations highlight a glowing primary button, and job-application tools guess answers to questions the user never checked. When everything looks smooth, human oversight shrinks into passive muscle memory.

Across three recent builds — [Lamplight Nook](/projects/lamplight), [Resume Autofill](/projects/autofill), and [Walkie](/projects/walkie) — I treated deliberate refusal and asymmetrical friction as core interaction primitives.

## Why "100% auto-fill" is a false kindness

When someone applies to dozens of graduate positions, repeating work history across forms is genuinely exhausting. An autofiller that wants to impress users feels tempted to fill every visible field — calling an LLM to infer visa eligibility, salary expectations, or custom essay answers.

In [Resume Autofill](/projects/autofill), that temptation turns into asymmetric risk: the extension gets credit for convenience, while the applicant carries 100% of the legal and career consequence if a hallucinated compliance checkbox goes through.

So we engineered three explicit confidence lanes instead of one magic button:
1. Deterministic local profile matches fill immediately.
2. Ambiguous dropdowns pause for explicit human selection.
3. Sensitive or open-ended prompts remain blank by rule, and final form submission stays permanently locked out of software automation.

**Measured scope:** across five synthetic fixture pages matching local test patterns, mapping hit cleanly; real-world ATS DOM drift outside Lewis's own daily runs has not yet been sampled.

## Blocking habitual Enter-key fatigue

A similar failure mode appears when supervising long-running coding agents. Text logs stream faster than a person can verify, and standard desktop dialogs often default focus to **Continue / Proceed** so pressing `Return` keeps work moving.

In [Lamplight Nook](/projects/lamplight), staging an agent task as a spatial café crew is never meant to invite blind cuteness-driven trust; its blackboard separates *plan steps* from *execution risks*, and every destructive file overwrite or command card locks initial focus onto **Not now**. Dismissing the card refuses execution rather than silently proceeding.

Likewise on [Walkie](/projects/walkie), hardware buttons create tactile asymmetry: background speech triggers only an audio summary, while destructive tool permissions pause until a deliberate thumb press on the M5StickS3 Front or Side key (tested across automated relay & device gates on Lewis's watch, prior to external wearer studies).

## Intentionally un-automated

Good human-centered AI design declares clearly what the machine **refuses to guess or trigger silently**. Protecting cognitive sovereignty means designing calm friction right before irreversible commitments — even if that costs one extra click.
