Portfolios regularly overclaim clean metrics—claiming a slider or AI button improved speed without noting what broke under noisy inputs. My assumption, not something I have data on, is that experienced interviewers probe the brittle edges first.

## Contrasting pitch traps with codebase receipts

In the Interview Defense Arena across [/hire](/hire) and [/ai](/ai), every hiring track pairs a skeptical pushback question with two contrasting responses:

- **Rejected Trap**: Fabricated uplift figures or hand-wavy model prompt promises.
- **Evidence-Bounded Defense**: fail-closed rules that exist in the project code and tests, such as [Walkie](/projects/walkie)'s two-key approval and [Lamplight Nook](/projects/lamplight)'s default focus on "Not now", stated next to the field evidence that is still missing.
- **Regional framing**: the arena frames some questions for APAC teams (performance and closed-loop delivery) and some for ANZ teams (compliance and accessibility). These framings are my assumptions about what each market asks, not research findings.
