Portfolios regularly overclaim clean metrics—claiming a slider or AI button improved speed without noting what broke under noisy inputs. Experienced interviewers in Sydney, Melbourne, or Singapore probe the brittle edges first.

## Contrasting pitch traps with codebase receipts

In the Interview Defense Arena across [/hire](/hire) and [/ai](/ai), every hiring track pairs a skeptical pushback question with two contrasting responses:

- **Rejected Trap**: Fabricated uplift figures or hand-wavy model prompt promises.
- **Evidence-Bounded Defense**: Concrete fail-closed rules verified in hardware firmware, snapshot rollback code, and unit tests, plus explicit unverified field gaps.
- **Regional framing parity**: APAC tracks weigh system throughput and telemetry guardrails alongside ANZ expectations around WCAG 2.2 accessibility and calm withdrawal.
