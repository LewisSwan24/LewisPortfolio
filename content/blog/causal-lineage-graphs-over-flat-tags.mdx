Traditional portfolios flatten years of iteration into disconnected filter chips. A visitor sees a finished gadget or dashboard without ever learning which abandoned assumption forced its core constraint.

## Computing lineage server-side with accessible DOM parity

On [/studio/graph](/studio/graph), every artifact builds cleanly into four typed tiers—**8 Vault Autopsies → 6 Listed Flagships → 6 Interaction Patterns → 4 Monograph Clusters**:

- **Honest gap overlay**: Toggling unproven-gap filters highlights claims still awaiting longitudinal field studies rather than hiding uncertainty.
- **Keyboard and screen-reader symmetry**: Alongside interactive cubic Bezier links sits a synchronized HTML adjacency table with arrow navigation.
- **Zero route bloat**: Graph layout resolves on the server while keeping the client JS bundle at roughly 5 kB.
