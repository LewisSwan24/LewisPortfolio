Purely decorative 3D canvases look slick for three seconds before feeling detached from real engineering behavior. When a case talks about USB preemption or infrared debouncing, a visitor should be able to see the state change and the component it belongs to at the same time.

## Persistent WebGL state without context churn

In [/studio/replay](/studio/replay), `HardwareFieldWebGL` reuses a single mounted `WebGLRenderer` across every frame step:

1. **In-place material mutation**: Stepping frames mutates zone color, opacity, and signal scale through a lightweight ref without tearing down Three.js scenes.
2. **Bidirectional subsystem jump**: Selecting `Dual-Key Approval Gate` or `FC-51 IR[04]` steps the byte inspector straight to the matching frame.
3. **Resilient 2D SVG parity**: On `webglcontextlost`, reduced-motion preferences, or explicit schematic toggle, an identical 2D SVG anchor frame takes over with zero console errors.

The frames themselves are hand-authored simulations of the firmware logic, not traces captured from the devices. The 3D view explains a rule; it is not evidence that the hardware behaved that way.
