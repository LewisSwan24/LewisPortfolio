Adding a service worker to an essay platform improves plane and commuter reading instantly, yet blanket caching breaks cache-control invariants when dynamic `/brief/[id]` snapshots or `/api/*` endpoints coexist on the same origin.

## Explicit fail-closed bypass rules alongside `worker-src 'self'`

In `v6.0.0`, `public/sw.js` ships with strict scoping rules verified against `next.config.js`:

- **Private route immunity**: Requests under `/api/` and `/brief/` (plus `/{en,zh}/brief/`) return directly to the browser network stack—never touched by Cache Storage.
- **No-cache worker updates**: `next.config.js` pairs `worker-src 'self'` CSP with `Cache-Control: no-cache, no-store, must-revalidate` on `/sw.js` so stale workers rotate reliably.
- **Network-first reading shell**: Public EN/ZH case pages and the 30 monographs gracefully serve last-visited HTML copies when connectivity drops.
