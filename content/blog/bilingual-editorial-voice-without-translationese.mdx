A bilingual portfolio serves two genuinely different technical audiences: ANZ and global Design Engineering teams who expect crisp, verb-first architectural critique, and APAC AI-native product teams who scan quickly for clear causal arcs—**User Pain → Mechanism → Engineering Loop → Honest Boundary**.

## Why literal translation breaks both audiences

Machine-translated English drifts into padded calques (`"carry out a research"`, `"realize the function of"`), while literal English-to-Chinese translation floods paragraphs with awkward passive clauses (`"当按钮被用户点击时"`, `"一个为设计师而设计的……"`). Both voices lose technical authority immediately.

## Enforcing native register at build time

Rather than trusting manual proofreading alone, this portfolio treats linguistic quality as an automated test gate inside `src/lib/i18n/editorial-voice.ts`:

1. **Independent transcreation over parallel syntax**: Every `RawResearchVault`, `RedTeamDeck`, `ReplayTrace`, and essay pair shares identical factual ceilings and timestamps while phrasing arguments natively per language.
2. **Automated anti-translationese linting**: Vitest invariants reject stiff passive ZH patterns and EN Chinglish calques across new research dossiers, red-team defenses, and monograph titles.
3. **Cross-surface parity**: You can compare both registers side-by-side in the [Custom Resume Composer](/resume/compose), [AI Lab](/ai), and [Archive Vault](/vault).
