## Overview

AI Interview Assistant is a web-based platform that leverages artificial intelligence to help job seekers prepare for interviews through realistic practice sessions and personalized feedback. The platform simulates various interview scenarios and provides detailed analysis of user responses.

This project was developed as part of my coursework on AI in Design at UQ, exploring how AI can be integrated thoughtfully into educational tools.

## Problem

Job seekers face significant challenges when preparing for interviews:

- Limited access to realistic practice scenarios
- Difficulty receiving quality, actionable feedback
- High cost of professional interview coaching
- Anxiety and uncertainty about performance
- Lack of personalized preparation resources

Traditional preparation methods like reading common questions or practicing with friends often fail to replicate the pressure and unpredictability of real interviews.

## Research

### Survey Study

I conducted a survey with 85 job seekers across different experience levels:

- 42 entry-level applicants
- 31 mid-career professionals
- 12 senior-level candidates

**Key findings:**
- 78% felt underprepared for their last interview
- 89% wanted more realistic practice opportunities
- 65% couldn't afford professional coaching
- 72% experienced significant interview anxiety

### Competitive Analysis

I analyzed 6 existing interview preparation platforms:

- Pramp (peer-to-peer practice)
- Interviewing.io (anonymous practice)
- Big Interview (video-based training)
- Yoodli (AI speech coach)
- Google Interview Warmup
- Vooma (AI mock interviews)

**Gap identified:** None combined realistic AI conversations with comprehensive feedback on content, delivery, and body language.

### User Interviews

I conducted in-depth interviews with 15 participants who had interviewed in the past 6 months:

- 8 successfully got job offers
- 7 were still searching

**Common pain points:**
- Unexpected questions throw them off
- Difficulty articulating accomplishments
- Nervousness affects performance
- Unclear what interviewers are looking for

## Insights

### Authenticity Over Perfection

Users wanted realistic practice that felt human, not robotic. Overly polished AI responses felt discouraging rather than helpful.

### Feedback Timing Matters

Users preferred receiving feedback immediately after responses rather than at the end of sessions. Delayed feedback felt disconnected from the experience.

### Anxiety is a Skill Barrier

Many users knew what to say but anxiety prevented them from performing. Practice needed to address both content and confidence.

## Ideation

### Design Sprint

I facilitated a 5-day design sprint with 3 potential users and 2 career counselors:

**Day 1 - Map:** Defined the critical user journey from anxiety to confidence
**Day 2 - Sketch:** Individual solution sketching using Crazy 8s method
**Day 3 - Decide:** Selected features through dot voting and feasibility matrix
**Day 4 - Prototype:** Built high-fidelity prototype in Figma
**Day 5 - Test:** Conducted 5 user tests with target users

### Feature Prioritization

Through the sprint, we identified core features:

1. AI interviewer with customizable persona
2. Real-time speech analysis and feedback
3. Question bank with industry-specific sets
4. Progress tracking and skill improvement metrics
5. Optional video recording for self-review

## Prototype

### Low-Fidelity

Paper prototypes exploring conversation flow and information architecture. Key decisions:
- Chat-based interface felt most natural
- Progress indicators reduced anxiety
- Optional video was preferred over mandatory

### Mid-Fidelity

Figma wireframes with 3 interview scenarios:
- Behavioral questions ("Tell me about a time...")
- Technical questions (role-specific)
- Case study questions (problem-solving)

### High-Fidelity

Interactive prototype featuring:
- Realistic AI interviewer persona "Alex"
- Voice input with speech-to-text
- Real-time feedback on pace, filler words, and clarity
- Post-session detailed analysis
- Personalized improvement recommendations

## Testing

### Usability Testing

I conducted 12 moderated usability sessions over 2 weeks:

**Task Success Rates:**
- Complete a practice interview: 100%
- Review feedback and insights: 91.7%
- Access personalized recommendations: 83.3%

**Key Issues:**
- AI responses felt too fast and unnatural
- Feedback was sometimes overly critical
- Users wanted more control over interview length

### A/B Testing

I tested two feedback presentation styles:
- **Version A:** Immediate inline feedback
- **Version B:** End-of-session summary

**Results:** Version B had 34% higher satisfaction scores. Users found inline feedback distracting during the interview flow.

### Iteration

Based on testing insights, I implemented:

- Added natural pauses to AI responses
- Softened feedback language with constructive framing
- Introduced interview length presets (5/10/20 minutes)
- Added "confidence boost" mode with easier questions
- Implemented progress celebration moments

## Outcome

### Metrics

After 4 weeks of beta testing with 50 users:

- **84%** reported increased interview confidence
- **4.5/5** average platform satisfaction
- **28%** reduction in self-reported interview anxiety
- **3.2** average practice sessions per user

### User Testimonials

> "I went from freezing up in interviews to feeling prepared and confident. The AI feedback was surprisingly helpful." — Sarah M., Marketing Professional

> "Being able to practice at 11 PM when my anxiety was highest was a game-changer." — James L., Recent Graduate

## Reflection

### What Worked Well

- AI integration felt natural and helpful, not gimmicky
- User-centered approach ensured real problem-solving
- Iterative testing caught critical usability issues early

### Lessons Learned

- AI interfaces need careful calibration of personality and pacing
- Feedback delivery is as important as feedback content
- Emotional aspects of user experience are crucial for learning tools

### Future Directions

If continuing development:

- Integration with LinkedIn for job-specific preparation
- Peer comparison metrics for benchmarking
- Virtual reality for immersive interview environments
- Industry-specific AI interviewers (tech, healthcare, finance)
